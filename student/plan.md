## Why this plan
- Grammar accuracy (GRA 4) holds his Writing and Speaking down, so G8, G7, G3 and G6 come first and come back every day.
- Reading is already strong, so Reading and Listening types are taught quickly; Listening gets more time because long listening is weak.
- Ideas are thin, so every essay practises point, explanation, example (W2c), and each essay gets one paragraph rewrite.
- Short timed tasks twice a week build speed without losing accuracy; he is fast but careless, so accuracy checks matter.
