# My Mistakes

Repeated mistakes, grouped by type. Each has a wrong example, the correction, and how many times it happened.

## Grammar

- **Plural nouns / missing -s on "student":** "student should learn" → "students should learn" (essay, many times; also "when student learn")
- **Subject-verb agreement:** "Some thinks" → "Some people think"; "Kathmandu have got" → "Kathmandu has got"; "Many students travels" → "travel" (dictation); "it will makes me" → "it will make me" (4+ times)
- **Irregular past tense:** "teached" → "taught" (2 times in speaking Part 2)
- **Verb as subject:** "learn to cook at school is necessary" → "learning to cook at school is necessary"
- **Many / much:** "so much disadvantages" → "so many disadvantages"
- **Articles:** "at university" → "at a university" (dictation); missing articles in essay; 2026-10-04: "in future" → "in the future", "for promotion" → "for a promotion", "in city like Kathmandu" → "in a city like Kathmandu" (he knows the a/the rule when asked; forgets when writing)
- **Present habit with "will":** "I will spend time with my family" → "I spend / I usually spend" (when talking about habits)
- **Reported questions (test):** "He asked me where do I live" → "where I lived"
- **Past continuous (test):** "I did my homework when..." → "I was doing my homework when..."
- **Future perfect (test):** "will finish" → "will have finished"
- **Word order in indirect question:** "no matter what time is it" → "what time it is"

## Vocabulary and word choice

- "Despite of" → "Despite" / "In spite of"
- "come school" → "come to school"; "aside of" → "aside from"
- "hopping cafes" → "café-hopping / going to cafés"
- "for live" → "for life"
- "Although ... but": only one linking word is needed (self-corrected in dictation, check again in writing)

## Spelling

- schenario → scenario; statonaries → stationery; themself → themselves; mathmatics/mathmatical → mathematics/mathematical; specially → especially; soo → so; "6" written for "six" in dictation (check which form to use)
- Capital letters for names: "baker's street" → "Baker Street"; "kathmandu" → "Kathmandu"; "i" → "I" (2026-10-04: slipped 3 times in class: Kathmandu x2, Nepali; habit problem, teach a capital-letter check before sending)

- 2026-10-05: quite → quiet (wrong word, similar spelling); "over polluted" → "heavily polluted"; "Baker's street" → "Baker Street" (needed the answer shown); "homeworks" → "homework" (uncountable)

## Writing structure

- Under-developed ideas: one idea + explanation + example needed in each paragraph
- Long sentences with 3 ideas ("increase ... and increase ... which can improve ...") → split into shorter sentences
- Conclusion added a confusing new point ("so much disadvantages") instead of summarising why the advantages win

## Reading / Listening traps

- Dictation: missed the end of long sentences ("remains a serious problem for the environment"); practise listening to the whole sentence, then writing
