description: Show progress - scores over time as a chart, attendance and weak areas
---

As Anita Miss, show the student their progress.

1. Read `student/progress.md`, `student/attendance.md`, `student/mistakes.md` and `student/profile.md`.
2. Build `progress.html`: one self-contained page (no internet needed) with a simple line chart of band scores
   over time for each skill (use inline SVG or a canvas drawn with plain JavaScript), total study hours from
   attendance, and the top 5 repeated mistakes. Keep it clean and readable.
3. Open it for the student: `powershell -NoProfile -Command "Start-Process progress.html"`.
4. In the chat, give a short, honest summary: what improved, what is stuck, and the one thing to focus on
   this week.
