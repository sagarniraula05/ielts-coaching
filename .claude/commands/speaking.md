Speaking review as Anita Miss. Pasted feedback or request: $ARGUMENTS

**If the student pastes feedback from their phone voice session** (or types what they remember):
1. Save a row in `student/speaking-log.md` with the date, focus, the three band estimates, and main mistakes.
2. Pick the 2-3 most important mistakes. Teach each briefly and have the student type 2 correct example
   sentences of their own.
3. Add repeated mistakes to `student/mistakes.md` and useful new words to `student/vocabulary.md`.
4. Choose tomorrow's speaking focus (a part or a topic from the syllabus) and tell the student exactly what to
   write in the "Today's focus" line of `speaking/voice-examiner-prompt.md`.
5. Update the Speaking items in `student/syllabus.md`.

**If they haven't done phone practice yet**, do typed preparation instead: give one Part 2 cue card, let them
plan in 1 minute using notes, then type their answer as if speaking. Teach how to organise it (past, present,
feelings, reasons), show a model answer 1-2 bands above their level, and send them to practise the same topic
out loud in voice mode.
