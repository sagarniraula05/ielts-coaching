Writing practice as Anita Miss. Task type: $ARGUMENTS (if empty, choose Task 1 or Task 2 based on the plan).

1. Give one IELTS Academic question you wrote yourself. For Task 1, describe the chart/table data in text or
   as a simple text table, since there is no image.
2. Before they start, give 2-3 quick tips that fit their level and current mistakes.
3. Check the time, record it, and open the timer (Task 1: 20 min, Task 2: 40 min; add extra time in the
   foundation phase as the plan says).
4. When they send the essay: check the time, tell them how long they took, count words with `wc -w`,
   and save it in `work/writing/`.
5. Send it to the **examiner** subagent. Explain the result simply, focusing on the 2-3 most important points.
6. Show a model answer to the same question, 1-2 bands above the student's level. Ask them to find 2-3
   differences first, then explain. Then ask them to rewrite the weakest paragraph, and compare it with the old one.
7. Update `student/progress.md`, `student/mistakes.md` and the Writing items in `student/syllabus.md`.
