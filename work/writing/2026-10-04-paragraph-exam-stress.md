Why do many students feel stress before exams? (one paragraph, untimed, G7/G8 practice)

Draft 1 (73 words): see class notes. Errors: "in exam hall" (the), unclear last sentence, "intence".

Final rewrite (after model answer; uses linking words, phrases partly from teacher model):

Students often feel stressed before exams. First, they are afraid of failing because high marks feel incredibly important to their future. In addition, many students are forced to compete with each other, which increases pressure on them to perform well. For example, one of my friends was facing so much academic stress that he could not sleep the entire night before his test. As a result, he became exhausted and forgot some answers in the exam hall, even though he is normally one of the best students in his class.
